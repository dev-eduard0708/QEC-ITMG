# ISA 315–oriented IT audit profile

Related: [AUDIT-MANAGEMENT.md](../03-modules/AUDIT-MANAGEMENT.md) · [CONTROL-MODEL.md](CONTROL-MODEL.md) · [COBIT-STRATEGY.md](COBIT-STRATEGY.md)

## Purpose

Support **IT-related audit readiness** aligned with an **ISA 315–oriented** understanding of IT: how IT systems and controls affect the entity’s financial reporting risk assessment (understanding the entity and its environment, including IT).

This is an **audit profile / questionnaire and evidence packaging lens**, not a claim that ITMG performs statutory audits or replaces the external auditor.

## What it is

- Structured ITGC / application-control oriented questions and evidence requests
- Mapping of those requests to InternalControls and Evidence
- Export packs suitable for engagement responses

## What it is not

- Not a substitute for COBIT governance mapping
- Not ISO/IEC 27001 certification
- Not automatic “audit passed” scoring

## Relationship to COBIT

COBIT organizes **IT governance and management objectives**. ISA 315–oriented profiles organize **auditor understanding of IT** for financial-statement risk. Both may cite the same InternalControls and Evidence; roll-ups remain separate.
